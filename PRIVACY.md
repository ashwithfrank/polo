# Privacy Policy for Polo

**App:** Polo (`com.aos.polo`), an Android file manager and toolbox by AlphaOmega Studio
**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

## Summary
Polo works on your device only. We do not collect, store, share or sell your personal data.

## No internet
Polo does not request internet permission and does not connect to any server. Nothing you open, convert, scan or read is uploaded anywhere.

## Your files
Polo opens and changes files only when you ask it to. File names and contents are never sent to anyone. Conversion, compression, trimming, PDF tools, ZIP tools and text reading all run on your phone.

## Passwords
Passwords you type for the Vault, PDF lock and unlock, and protected ZIP files are used on your device to do that task. They are not stored by Polo (except the Vault's password check, kept as a salted hash on your device) and are never sent anywhere. A forgotten Vault, PDF or ZIP password cannot be recovered by us.

## Permissions
- **All files access / storage:** needed to browse and manage your files.
- **Camera:** used only while you use the QR scanner, text from a picture, or the document scanner. Scans stay on your device.
- **Vibration:** used only for tap feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Google ML Kit
Document scanning and text reading use Google ML Kit, which runs on your device (document scanning is provided through Google Play services). Polo does not send your images to Google.

## Stored on your device
Settings (theme, sort order, save location, vibration, favourites and recently opened list) are saved on your device. Deleted files go to a hidden "Polo trash" folder and are removed after 30 days, or sooner if you empty it. Clearing Polo's data or uninstalling it removes these settings.

## Sharing
When you share a file, the app you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Children
Polo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear here.
