# Polo Privacy Policy
 
Last updated: 14 November 2026
 
Polo is a file manager app for Android made by AlphaOmega Studio ("we", "us").
 
**Summary:** Polo works on your device only. We do not collect, store, share or sell your personal data.
 
## What Polo does with your files
Polo asks for access to your device's files so it can list, open, copy, move, rename, share, compress and delete them at your request. Everything happens on your device. Your files and file names are never sent to us or to anyone else.
 
## Information we collect
None. Polo does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports. Polo has no accounts and shows no ads.
 
## Network access
Polo does not request internet permission and does not connect to any server.
 
## Data stored on your device
Polo saves a few settings on your device, such as your theme, sort order and list or grid choice. Deleted files are moved to a hidden "Polo trash" folder on your device and are removed after 30 days, or earlier if you empty the trash. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall Polo, except for any files you chose to keep.
 
## Sharing files
If you use Share, the file is handed to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy applies from then on.
 
## Children
Polo does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
 
## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the new version will be published here.
